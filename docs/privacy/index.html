Vedra privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Versão em português

Summary: Vedra does not collect, store off your device or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission.

Who we are

Vedra is an Android app developed by AuraDev (auradevcode.com.br). It locks the apps you choose with a PIN or fingerprint.

Use of the Accessibility service

To lock an app the instant it opens, Vedra uses the Android Accessibility API (AccessibilityService). Through it, Vedra receives only the package name of the app in the foreground (for example, com.whatsapp).

  • Vedra does not read screen content, messages, text, photos, passwords, taps or typing.
  • The foreground app name is used only to decide whether that app should be locked. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Vedra does not use Accessibility to click, type or act in other apps.
  • You can turn the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

Data stored on the device

  • PIN: stored only as a cryptographic hash (PBKDF2 with an Android Keystore key that never leaves the device). The PIN itself is never stored.
  • List of protected apps and settings (such as the relock rule).
  • Wrong attempt count, for the security delay.

This data lives only in the app's private storage, is excluded from cloud backup and is deleted when you uninstall Vedra or clear its data.

Biometrics

Fingerprints are verified by Android itself. Vedra never receives or stores your fingerprint; it only gets the result (success or failure) from the system.

Installed apps list

So you can choose what to protect, Vedra lists the apps that have a launcher icon. This list is used only on the device.

Sharing and third parties

Vedra has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party libraries that collect data. Since it has no internet permission, it cannot send anything off the device.

Children

Vedra is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
